# Ridebly

Ridebly is a business-management and online-booking platform for companies offering vehicle
rentals, private transfers, and shuttle services. It brings together the operational side
(vehicles, availability, pricing and reservations) with the customer-facing booking experience.

In one sentence: Ridebly helps rental, transfer and shuttle businesses manage their vehicles and
bookings internally while letting customers book their services online.

## Main capabilities

- Vehicle rentals: manage fleets, rental availability, seasonal/daily rates, rental-duration
  discounts and booking requests.
- Transfers and shuttles: manage journeys, routes, stops, schedules and fares.
- Reservations: handle customer details, vehicle assignments, booking statuses and related
  notifications.
- Online booking: provide customer booking forms and website widgets, using the business's
  configured prices and availability.
- Fleet operations: track vehicle information, maintenance, damages and odometer records.
- Business administration: manage staff permissions, partners/colleagues, commissions, payments
  and invoicing.

Ridebly supports different business types, so features and workflows vary. Rental pricing uses a
vehicle/date-based Rate Manager; transfer and shuttle pricing uses journey or route fares. Access
also depends on permissions and enabled business packages.

## Plans and pricing (from ridebly.com/pricing; always show the live page for exact prices)

- Monthly subscription based on fleet size, no per-booking commission, 14-day free trial with no
  card and no commitment.
- Tourist transport (transfers, shuttles): price depends on the number of vehicles and users,
  from €19.99/month: plan N covers N vehicles and 2N users (1 vehicle/2 users €19.99 up to
  20 vehicles/40 users); custom plan for more than 20 vehicles. The booking form is an add-on
  that costs €30 (the website doesn't say whether that is once or per month, so don't say either).
  Seasonal businesses only pay in the months they are active.
- Rent a car: €2.49 per vehicle per month for 1 to 100 vehicles; custom plan above 100. The
  booking form is included free.
- Every plan: unlimited trips or rentals, the Ridebly app for office and phone, free initial setup
  by the Ridebly team, support 09:00-23:00. Annual billing saves 10%.
- Optional: shuttle with scheduled departures (enabled on request for tourist transport), online
  payments through the business's own Viva or Nexi account.
